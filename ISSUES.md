# Issues — Card Collector v2

> Architecture plan for the style/pipeline unification: [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).
> Its phase 2 (shared shell + control kit) closes most of the style-drift items below wholesale.

## Open
- [ ] **Levelers and the Unstable joke tokens still clip, and they are on the standard path** — the 14 remaining overflows out of 411 anatomy samples are not the rebuilt frames: `leveler | framed` (3 of 6, worst 22px) and `double_faced_token | framed` (6 of 12, worst 308px) both render through the ordinary `CardSide` at `room: 1`, so they clip for the same reason an ordinary card with 1,489 characters of oracle text does — the ladder bottoms out at `0.55em` rather than pretending. The 308px case is *Strictly Better // Strictly Better (cont'd)*, an Unstable card that is a deliberate wall of text and will not fit at any readable size. **The Leveler half is half fixed**: banding them (resolved 2026-08-15) took the same 26 cards in the same box from 22 overflowing to 11, worst 178px → 157px, because two bars replace six paragraphs — but 11 still overflow, so the remaining lever is `textFit`'s ladder rather than the frame. Not urgent — this is the documented tail, not a regression. *(found 2026-08-14, halved 2026-08-15)*
- [ ] **The booster opens with no wrapper and no motion** — asked for explicitly as a later step: the 2D pack art manipulated to read as being torn open, then the cards animating face-down into their two rows before you flip them. The pieces are all present — `PACK_ART` already has the wrapper photo per set and per booster variant, the cards are already dealt whole from a seed before anything is shown, and the reveal is already per-card — so this is presentation over a model that already holds still. Worth doing after the window earns its keep, not before. *(found 2026-08-12)*
- [ ] **Binder pages no longer skip layout while offscreen** — `content-visibility:auto` needed `contain-intrinsic-size` in px, and the binder is now sized entirely in fractions of the pane, so there is no px height to declare. Dropped rather than faked with a magic number. It is not currently load-bearing: `CARD_LIMIT` caps the view at 240 cards, so the worst case is ~240 pages (grouping by collector number), not the 11,802 Unsorted would otherwise want. Worth restoring the moment the cap is lifted — either by measuring the pane once and stashing it in `P` (`measureGutter` already does a post-render measure), or by giving the page an `aspect-ratio` so its block size is definite under containment. *(found 2026-08-12)*
- [ ] **The 186px booster square pads the set table with 549 blank rows** — the art is sized by the picture, so a block shorter than 7 rows is padded to fit it (`PACK_ROWS = 7`, a row being 33px at its shortest — 6 for the square, +1 for the Draft chip that now sits above it). At the old 93px that cost 102 gap rows; at 186px it costs **549**, and the table goes 986 → 1535 rows, a third of it blank. It renders fine and the alignment is right — the cost is that a set with no sub-sets now sits in a tall empty band. Levers, cheapest first: a smaller square (124px = 4 rows would roughly halve it), letting short blocks share one gap row sized to the shortfall instead of N whole rows, or dropping the square for the wrapper's own 0.525 aspect. *(found 2026-08-11)*
- [ ] **The deal has no "Skip to End", and "Reveal all" should not exist until the cards have landed** — the capture is explicit: while the cards are being dealt the button reads **Skip to End**, and only once they are all down does it become **Reveal All**. Ours offers Reveal all throughout, including during the fly, where pressing it fights the animation. Needs a `P.draw.dealing` flag set across the deal, the button swapping on it, and Skip jumping every ghost to its slot at once. *(found 2026-08-13)*
